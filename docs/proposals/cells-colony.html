Colonies

One problem,
a whole team.

A single cell is one agent. A colony is what you get when one problem needs more than one — a structured team, with a Chief of Staff and specialists under it.

The unit

an organism

One agent in its own Linux machine. Named, persistent, with its own memory and identity.

Pods + a head

The flagship colony

Jurypool — a council of thirteen.

Jurypool is a deliberation colony: a foreman Chief of Staff, nine philosopher jurors, and a three-cell portal pod of builders. The nine jurors share one spec — they're differentiated only by the persona written into each one's SOUL.md. The whole colony was described once, then birthed cell by cell.

How it deliberates

Ask the foreman. Hear from nine minds.

A question to the foreman doesn't get one answer. It fans out to all nine jurors at once, each thinking in its own voice. The foreman pulls two or three threads deeper, then synthesizes a single verdict.

Every juror writes what it learned to its own memory after each deliberation; a nightly dream pass consolidates it. Over time, the jury learns you.

Conflict is a test of truth: meet it without hatred, with courage, and with means worthy of the peace you seek.

Not just jurypool

Colonies ship as patterns.

You don't wire a colony by hand. Describe the company you need; a Creator cell interviews you, births the colony from a pattern, and hibernates when it's done.
